🥔 Aloo Fry

Lunch
Ingredients
Potato1 kg
Onion200 g
Garlic6 cloves
Oil4 tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Cumin1 tsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Chilli powder1 tsp
Coriander powder1½ tsp
Salt2 tsp
Curry leaves1 sprig
Method
  1. Peel and cut potatoes into small cubes.
  2. Heat oil; add mustard, cumin, garlic and curry leaves.
  3. Add onion and sauté 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add potatoes, turmeric and salt.
  5. Cover and cook on low until nearly done.
  6. Add chilli and coriander powders.
  7. Fry uncovered until lightly crisp.
Pair well with Tomato Pappu or Drumstick Sambar.

🌿 Bendakaya Fry

Lunch
Ingredients
Bhindi900 g
Onion200 g
Oil4 tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Cumin1 tsp
Urad dal1 tsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Chilli powder¾–1 tsp
Coriander powder1 tsp
Salt1¾ tsp
Curry leaves1 sprig
Method
  1. Wash bhindi and dry completely before cutting.
  2. Heat oil and temper mustard, cumin, urad dal and curry leaves.
  3. Add onion and sauté lightly.
  4. Add bhindi and turmeric; cook mostly uncovered.
  5. When slime reduces, add salt and spice powders.
  6. Cook until tender and lightly crisp.
Drying bhindi thoroughly is the key to reducing sliminess.

🍅 Aloo Tomato Curry

Dinner
Ingredients
Potato800 g
Tomato500 g
Onion250 g
Ginger-garlic paste1 tbsp
Green chilli2
Oil3 tbsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Chilli powder¾ tsp
Coriander powder1½ tsp
Garam masala½ tsp
Water300 ml
Salt2 tsp
Method
  1. Cube and parboil potatoes.
  2. Sauté onion in oil until soft.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilli.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook until pulpy.
  5. Add spices and salt.
  6. Add potato and water; simmer 8–10 minutes.
  7. Finish with garam masala.

🥕 Beans-Carrot Coconut Fry

Dinner
Ingredients
Beans600 g
Carrot400 g
Grated coconut60–70 g
Onion150 g
Green chilli2
Oil2½ tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Urad dal1 tsp
Turmeric¼ tsp
Salt1¾ tsp
Curry leaves1 sprig
Method
  1. Finely chop beans and carrot.
  2. Temper mustard, urad dal, curry leaves and green chilli.
  3. Add onion briefly.
  4. Add beans, carrot, turmeric and salt.
  5. Sprinkle little water, cover and cook.
  6. Finish with grated coconut.

🍆 Vankaya Tomato Kura

Lunch
Ingredients
Brinjal900 g
Tomato400 g
Onion250 g
Ginger-garlic paste1 tbsp
Green chilli2
Oil3 tbsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Chilli powder¾–1 tsp
Coriander powder1½ tsp
Cumin powder½ tsp
Water150–200 ml
Salt2 tsp
Method
  1. Cut brinjal into medium pieces and keep in water.
  2. Sauté onion, then ginger-garlic and green chilli.
  3. Add tomato and cook down well.
  4. Add spice powders and salt.
  5. Add brinjal and water.
  6. Cover and cook until soft; reduce until masala coats pieces.

🥬 Cabbage-Carrot Kura

Dinner
Ingredients
Cabbage700 g
Carrot300 g
Onion150 g
Grated coconut50 g
Green chilli2
Oil2½ tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Chana dal1 tsp
Urad dal1 tsp
Turmeric¼ tsp
Salt1¾ tsp
Method
  1. Shred cabbage and grate or finely cut carrot.
  2. Temper mustard, chana dal and urad dal.
  3. Add chilli and onion briefly.
  4. Add cabbage, carrot, turmeric and salt.
  5. Cover and cook until just tender.
  6. Add coconut and cook 1–2 minutes.

Lunch
Ingredients

Roasted peanuts120 g
Sesame seeds25–30 g
Dry coconut40 g
Onion200 g
Tamarind20 g
Oil4 tbsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Chilli powder1 tsp
Coriander powder1½ tsp
Cumin1 tsp
Salt2 tsp
Method
  1. Roast and grind peanuts, sesame and dry coconut.
  2. Slit brinjals and keep in water.
  3. Sauté onion in oil.
  4. Add brinjals, turmeric and spices.
  5. Add tamarind extract and ground peanut mix.
  6. Add enough water for gravy; cook covered until soft.

🍌 Raw Banana Fry

Lunch
Ingredients
Raw banana1 kg
Garlic5 cloves
Oil4 tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Cumin1 tsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Chilli powder1 tsp
Coriander powder1 tsp
Salt2 tsp
Method
  1. Peel and cube raw bananas.
  2. Parboil or steam to about 70% cooked.
  3. Temper mustard, cumin, garlic and curry leaves.
  4. Add banana, turmeric and salt.
  5. Fry until edges begin to colour.
  6. Add spice powders and fry until lightly crisp.

🥦 Mixed Vegetable Kurma

Dinner
Ingredients
Potato250 g
Carrot200 g
Beans150 g
Green peas150 g
Cauliflower250 g
Onion250 g
Tomato200 g
Coconut80 g
Cashew20 g
Ginger-garlic paste1 tbsp
Oil3 tbsp
Garam masala1 tsp
Method
  1. Chop vegetables evenly.
  2. Grind coconut and cashew into a smooth paste.
  3. Sauté onion, then ginger-garlic and tomato.
  4. Add turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder and salt.
  5. Add vegetables and cook with enough water.
  6. Add coconut-cashew paste and garam masala; simmer 5–7 minutes.

Dinner
Ingredients

Onion200 g
Tomato200 g
Green chilli2
Moong dal (optional)50 g
Oil2 tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Cumin1 tsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Salt1¾ tsp
Method
  1. Peel and cube bottle gourd.

🥘 Bendakaya Pulusu

Lunch
Ingredients
Bhindi750–800 g
Onion250 g
Tomato200 g
Tamarind35 g
Jaggery15 g
Oil3 tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Fenugreek¼ tsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Chilli powder1 tsp
Coriander powder1½ tsp
Water700 ml
Method
  1. Shallow-fry cut bhindi lightly and set aside.
  2. Temper mustard, fenugreek and curry leaves.
  3. Add onion, then tomato.
  4. Add tamarind extract, spices, jaggery and salt.
  5. Boil until raw tamarind smell reduces.
  6. Add fried bhindi and simmer 8–10 minutes.

🍆 Gutti Vankaya-Style Curry

Lunch
Ingredients
Small brinjals1 kg
Roasted peanuts100 g
Sesame seeds30 g
Coconut40 g
Onion200 g
Tamarind20 g
Oil4 tbsp
Chilli powder1 tsp
Coriander powder1½ tbsp
Cumin1 tsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Salt2 tsp
Method
  1. Roast peanuts, sesame and coconut; grind with spices and salt.
  2. Slit brinjals in a cross without separating the base.
  3. Stuff with masala.
  4. Place gently in hot oil.
  5. Add remaining masala and little tamarind water.
  6. Cover and cook on low until soft, turning gently.

Garlic5 cloves

Chilli powder1 tsp
Coriander powder1 tsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Salt2 tsp
Method
  1. Slice dondakaya thinly and crush peanuts coarsely.

🥣 Mixed Vegetable Moong Dal Curry

Dinner
Ingredients
Bottle gourd300 g
Carrot150 g
Spinach200 g
Tomato150 g
Moong dal250 g
Garlic6 cloves
Green chilli2
Cumin1 tsp
Turmeric½ tsp
Ghee/oil2 tbsp
Salt2 tsp
Method
  1. Wash moong dal and chop vegetables.
  2. Pressure cook dal, bottle gourd, carrot, tomato, turmeric and chilli.
  3. Mash lightly.
  4. Add spinach and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Temper cumin and crushed garlic in ghee/oil.
  6. Pour into dal and adjust consistency.

Dal Rotation

Four useful pappu options for the weekday table.

Tomato Pappu

Toor dal250 g
Tomato400 g
Green chilli2–3
Turmeric½ tsp
Tamarind10–15 g optional
Garlic5 cloves
Method
  1. Pressure cook dal, tomato, chilli and turmeric.
  2. Mash and add salt plus optional tamarind.
  3. Simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Temper mustard, cumin, red chilli, garlic and curry leaves in ghee/oil.

Palakura Pappu

Toor dal250 g
Spinach400 g
Tomato200 g
Green chilli2
Tamarind15 g
Garlic6 cloves
Method
  1. Cook dal, spinach, tomato, chilli and turmeric together.
  2. Mash well.
  3. Add tamarind and salt; simmer.
  4. Finish with garlic-mustard-cumin tempering.

Dosakaya Pappu

Toor dal250 g
Dosakaya500 g
Tomato150 g
Green chilli3
Tamarind20 g
Turmeric½ tsp
Method
  1. Peel and cube dosakaya.
  2. Pressure cook with dal, tomato, chilli and turmeric.
  3. Mash lightly.
  4. Add tamarind and salt, then finish with tempering.

Gongura Pappu

Toor dal250 g
Gongura leaves250–300 g
Onion150 g
Green chilli3
Garlic6 cloves
Turmeric½ tsp
Method
  1. Cook dal with turmeric until soft.
  2. Cook gongura separately until wilted.
  3. Mash and combine.
  4. Finish with garlic tempering.

Sambar Rotation

Three versatile sambars to rotate with dry vegetable dishes.

Drumstick Sambar

Toor dal250 g
Drumsticks3
Tomato250 g
Onion/shallots150 g
Tamarind30 g
Sambar powder2½ tbsp
Water1.5 L
Method
  1. Cook and mash dal.
  2. Boil drumstick, onion and tomato in tamarind water.
  3. Add turmeric, salt and sambar powder.
  4. Add dal and simmer 8–10 minutes.
  5. Finish with mustard, red chilli, hing and curry leaves tempering.

Onion-Tomato Sambar

Toor dal250 g
Onion300 g
Tomato300 g
Tamarind30 g
Sambar powder2½ tbsp
Water1.5 L
Method
  1. Cook and mash dal.
  2. Boil onion and tomato in tamarind water.
  3. Add turmeric, salt and sambar powder.
  4. Add dal and simmer.
  5. Finish with standard sambar tempering.

Vegetable Sambar

Toor dal250 g
Drumstick1
Carrot150 g
Pumpkin200 g
Brinjal150 g
Tomato200 g
Tamarind30 g
Sambar powder2½ tbsp
Method
  1. Cook dal until soft.
  2. Boil vegetables in tamarind water.
  3. Add turmeric, salt and sambar powder.
  4. Add mashed dal and simmer until flavours combine.
  5. Finish with tempering.

Simple Tempering Base

Oil or ghee1½–2 tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Cumin½–1 tsp
Dry red chilli2
Curry leaves1 sprig
Hing1 pinch
Method
  1. Heat oil/ghee.
  2. Crackle mustard and cumin.
  3. Add dry chilli, curry leaves and hing.
  4. Pour immediately over dal or sambar.
